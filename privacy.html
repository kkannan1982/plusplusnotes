Privacy Policy

++Notes · Effective October 29, 2026

Support Privacy Policy
++Notes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Your notes

Everything you write is saved only on your Mac, as plain Markdown files inside the app's own sandboxed Documents folder. ++Notes never uploads, syncs, or shares your notes. If you use a backup or sync service of your own (such as Time Machine), it handles those files under its own terms.

No tracking

++Notes has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting services, or third-party SDKs, and it makes no network connections.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
